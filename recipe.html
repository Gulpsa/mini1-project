Vegetable Sandwich

A vegetable sandwich is a simple, tasty and healthy snack.

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Butter
  • Tomato
  • Cucumber
  • Onion
  • Salt and Pepper

How to Make

  1. Take two slices of bread.
  2. Apply butter on the bread.
  3. Add tomato, cucumber and onion.
  4. Sprinkle salt and pepper.
  5. Cover with another bread slice.
  6. Cut and serve the sandwich.

Recipe Details

Preparation Time: 10 Minutes

Cooking Time: 5 Minutes

Category: Snacks
